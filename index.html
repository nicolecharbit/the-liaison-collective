STUDIO & PLATFORM OVERVIEW

Behind The Liaison Collective

The Liaison Collective functions as a digital creative studio and strategy lab founded by Nicole Charbit. Born out of a passion for music culture, editorial media, and high-fashion branding, the platform acts as the original, innovative and professional link between emerging talent, brand identity, and multi-channel campaign execution.

Grounded across global trends and cultures, the studio approaches marketing not as simple promotion, but as immersive and original storytelling. Across this interactive platform, you will explore artist launch plans, experiential brand spec pitch decks, original editorial graphic designs, and creative vision treatments.
